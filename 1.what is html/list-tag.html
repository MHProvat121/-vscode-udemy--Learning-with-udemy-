Angela's recipe for the best cinnamon rolls

Ingredients

For the dough

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 large egg

For the filling:

  • ½ cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter, softened

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine warm milk, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes until frothy.
  2. Add melted butter and egg to the yeast mixture and mix well.
  3. Gradually add flour and salt, mixing until a dough forms.
  4. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it, and let it rise for about 1 hour or until doubled in size.
  6. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
  7. Roll out the dough into a rectangle on a floured surface.
  8. Spread the softened butter over the dough, then sprinkle the brown sugar and cinnamon evenly on top.
  9. Roll up the dough tightly from one long side to the other and cut into 12 equal pieces.
  10. Place the rolls in a greased baking dish, cover, and let them rise for another 30 minutes.
  11. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown.
  12. Let cool slightly before serving. Enjoy your delicious cinnamon rolls!
